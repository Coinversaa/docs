All accounts start on **Free**. Upgrade in the [billing portal](https://developers.coinversa.ai/billing) when you outgrow it.

## Tiers

| Tier | Rate / min | Per day | Per month | Active keys | Monthly | Yearly (~17% off) |
|------|-----------:|--------:|----------:|------------:|--------:|------------------:|
| Free       | 30   | 1,000   | unlimited | 1   | $0     | —      |
| Starter    | 120  | 2,000   | 50,000    | 3   | $29    | $290   |
| Agent      | 300  | 10,000  | 250,000   | 5   | $49    | $490   |
| Pro        | 600  | 20,000  | 500,000   | 10  | $199   | $1,990 |
| Enterprise | 10,000+ | unlimited | unlimited | unlimited | from $1,999 | custom quote |

The websocket is metered separately, on **capacity** rather than requests — streaming never draws down the numbers above. Connections are capped **per account** (shared across all your keys), subscriptions per connection, and bandwidth is a fair-use allowance:

| Tier | Websocket connections (per account) | Subscriptions per connection | Feeds | Fair-use bandwidth |
|------|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Free       | 1 | 2   | `tradesByCoin`, `tradesByUser`, `candle`, `twapSliceFillsByUser`, `bbo`, `allMids` | 5 GB / mo |
| Starter    | 1 | 25  | Free's feeds plus `trades`, `tradesByUsers`, `allCandles`, `twapSliceFills`, `twapSliceFillsByUsers`, `l2Book` | 50 GB / mo |
| Agent      | 2 | 50  | Starter's feeds plus `liquidations`, `liquidationsByUser`, `liquidationsByUsers` | 100 GB / mo |
| Pro        | 5 | 100 | all 24 — Agent's feeds plus `l4Book`, leverage changes, TWAP orders and TWAP statuses | 250 GB / mo |
| Enterprise | custom | custom | all 24                 | 250 GB / mo |

Details in [WebSocket plan limits](/websocket/limits).

## How billing works

- Subscriptions are managed by Stripe. Manage payment methods, view invoices, change plan, and cancel in the [billing portal](https://developers.coinversa.ai/billing).
- Plan changes take effect immediately on upgrade (prorated) and at period end on downgrade.
- If your subscription lapses (failed payments keep access for up to 14 days), keys fall back to **Free** limits — they're not deleted, they just stop being entitled to the paid tier.
- Each key is entitlement-checked on its own: a key serves the tier it was provisioned at, capped by your account's active entitlement. A Free key is never promoted by a paid key on the same account — see [how tier resolution works](/api-reference/tiers#how-tier-resolution-works).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do you have annual billing?">
    Yes — Starter, Agent and Pro all have yearly cadence (~17% cheaper than 12× monthly). Pick "Yearly" on the checkout page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I need higher per-minute limits but lower volume?">
    That's Enterprise — limits are negotiated independently. Email [chat@coinversa.ai](mailto:chat@coinversa.ai).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are rate limits per key or per account?">
    REST RPM and daily/monthly usage are shared across keys on the same account. Additional keys do not create separate allocations. Access is still evaluated using the presented key's effective tier, capped by your account's entitlement — a Free key does not inherit a paid sibling's access.

    The **websocket connection cap is also per account**, shared across every key you own — five Pro connections are five in total, however many keys hold them. See [WebSocket plan limits](/websocket/limits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do MCP and direct API calls share the same limit?">
    Yes. The MCP server forwards your key to the API, so both paths consume the same quota.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
