A **Sharpe ratio** asks how steady a wallet's profits are, not how large. Pulse computes one for every tracked Hyperliquid wallet from its daily realized PnL — a single wallet's score, the same score over any window in the last 90 days, three month-sized buckets to see whether it is improving, and a leaderboard of the most consistent wallets with real equity behind them. All four endpoints are **Starter**.

## How the score is computed

```text
annualizedPnlSharpe = √365 × mean(daily realized PnL) ÷ sample standard deviation(daily realized PnL)
```

- **Daily realized PnL**, in dollars, from the same per-wallet daily rollup as the rest of Pulse. It is not limited to perps.
- **Every day in the window counts.** A day without fills counts as `0`, so a wallet that trades twice a month is not scored on its two good days.
- **Completed UTC days only.** Today is always excluded; `asOf` is midnight UTC at the end of the window, and `calculatedAt` is when the number was computed.
- **Risk-free rate is zero.** Annualized with √365 because crypto trades every day.
- **Null, not zero,** when there are fewer than two days or the daily PnL never varies. The score is rounded to 4 decimals.

<Warning>
  **This is a realized-PnL proxy, not a portfolio Sharpe.** It is not divided by account equity, so it measures dollar PnL rather than percentage returns, and it excludes **fees, funding and unrealized PnL**. A wallet sitting on a large open loss can score well.
</Warning>

**It rewards consistency, not size.** A wallet that makes $80 a day without fail can outrank one that made $1.5M with some red days — on the 14 Oct 2026 leaderboard, the #2 wallet had $77.85 of realized PnL over the 30 days. Always read `annualizedPnlSharpe` alongside `realizedPnl` and `trades`, which every response carries next to it with `meanDailyPnl` and `stdDailyPnl`.

## Endpoints

| Endpoint | MCP tool | What it returns | Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/pulse/trader/{address}/sharpe` | `pulse_trader_sharpe` | One wallet's score and its inputs | Last 30 completed UTC days (`days` accepts only `30`) |
| `/pulse/trader/{address}/sharpe/interval` | `pulse_trader_sharpe_interval` | The same, over a window you choose | `startDay` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, inclusive) and `days` (1–90), both required |
| `/pulse/trader/{address}/sharpe/monthly` | `pulse_trader_sharpe_monthly` | Exactly three buckets, newest first | 3 × 30 days, or calendar months with `useCalendarMonths=true` |
| `/pulse/sharpe-leaderboard` | `pulse_sharpe_leaderboard` | Top 50 wallets by score | Last 30 completed UTC days (`days` accepts only `30`) |

The wallet endpoints answer for wallets Pulse tracks; an address it has never indexed returns `404`. They apply **no equity filter** — any tracked wallet gets a score.

### Custom intervals

`/sharpe/interval` scores 1–90 completed days. `startDay` must be no earlier than 90 days before today, and the exclusive end (`startDay + days`) cannot be later than today. **Out-of-range windows are rejected with `400`, not shortened** — unlike the silent `since` clamps elsewhere in the API. A one-day interval returns a `null` score and a standard deviation of `0`.

### Monthly buckets

`/sharpe/monthly` always returns three buckets, newest first, each with its own `startDay`, `endDayExclusive`, `days` and `asOf`:

- **Default** (`useCalendarMonths=false`): the last 30 completed days, the 30 before that, and the 30 before that — 90 days in all.
- **`useCalendarMonths=true`**: the completed days of the current UTC month plus the two full calendar months before it — at most 93 days. On the 1st of a month the current bucket has `days: 0`, zero statistics and a `null` score.

### The leaderboard

`/pulse/sharpe-leaderboard` ranks the top **50** wallets. To be eligible a wallet must have fills in the window and **at least $2,500 of stored account equity** (`minEquity`); the equity check happens before ranking. Wallets with no equity on record, or with zero variance, are left out. Ties break by realized PnL, then by wallet address.

- `accountValue` and `equityUpdatedAt` are the wallet's **latest stored equity checkpoint** — not necessarily its live balance, and no checkpoint is too old to count.
- The result is **cached and refreshed every 30 minutes**; if a refresh fails, the previous result may be served. `calculatedAt` tells you how old the answer is.
- There is **no minimum-activity filter** beyond having fills in the window, which is why low-PnL wallets can rank high (see above).

## Sharpe filters on cohort endpoints

Nine cohort and positioning endpoints take two optional filters that keep only wallets above a Sharpe threshold:

| Parameter | Score it filters on | Refreshed |
|---|---|---|
| `minRecentSharpe` | The wallet's score over the last 30 completed UTC days | Hourly |
| `minTotalSharpe` | The wallet's score from its first available daily history through yesterday UTC | Daily |

Both are **inclusive minimums**. Zero and negative values are valid; non-finite values are `422`. Leave a filter out and nothing is filtered on it; set it and wallets without a score are excluded. Set both and a wallet must pass both. Filters apply **before** limits and aggregation, so totals and medians describe the qualifying wallets only.

The filter uses each wallet's **current** score. On trade and lifecycle history, a fill from three weeks ago is kept or dropped by the wallet's score today, not its score then — do not use it to backtest.

## What you can ask

| Question | Tool |
|---|---|
| Who are the most consistent traders on Hyperliquid this month? | `pulse_sharpe_leaderboard` |
| What's the Sharpe ratio of 0x…? | `pulse_trader_sharpe` |
| How did 0x…'s Sharpe look between 1 and 21 September? | `pulse_trader_sharpe_interval` |
| Is 0x… getting more or less consistent month by month? | `pulse_trader_sharpe_monthly` |
| How is BTC positioned among Apex wallets with a 30-day Sharpe above 2? | `live_cohort_bias` with `minRecentSharpe: 2` |
| Where did high-Sharpe wallets get into their ETH positions? | `live_positioning_by_entry` with `minTotalSharpe` |

```bash curl
curl "https://api.coinversa.ai/api/public/v1/pulse/trader/0xYOUR_WALLET/sharpe/interval?startDay=2026-09-01&days=21" \
  -H "X-API-Key: $COINVERSA_API_KEY"
```

## Coverage

- **Wallets**: every wallet Pulse tracks (see [Data coverage](/concepts/data-coverage)). The leaderboard additionally needs a stored equity checkpoint of at least $2,500.
- **History**: per-wallet scores and intervals reach back **90 days**; the monthly view up to 93. `minTotalSharpe` uses each wallet's full available daily history.
- **Freshness**: wallet scores are computed on each request from the daily rollup; the leaderboard is up to 30 minutes old; the stored filter scores refresh hourly (recent) and daily (total).
