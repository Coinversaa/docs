Live status and incident history for every Coinversa surface are on the **status page** at [status.coinversa.ai](https://status.coinversa.ai). Check it first when something looks wrong — and subscribe there to get incident updates by **email** or **RSS**.

## What is monitored

| Component | What it covers |
|-----------|----------------|
| **REST API** | `api.coinversa.ai/api/public/v1` — every endpoint, including the x402 twins |
| **MCP server** | The hosted MCP endpoint your AI client connects to |
| **Live data freshness** | Whether live positions, liquidations and market data are keeping up with Hyperliquid — see [Data coverage](/concepts/data-coverage) |
| **WebSocket streams** | `wss://ws.coinversa.ai` connections and feeds — see [WebSocket overview](/websocket/overview) |

## During an incident

The REST API returns a **5xx** status while a component is degraded. Treat any 5xx as retryable — **retry with exponential backoff** (with jitter) rather than in a tight loop, and check the status page before you escalate. A 4xx is not an incident: it is about the request or the key — see [Authentication](/authentication).

WebSocket clients should reconnect with backoff too; refused upgrades and close codes are covered in [WebSocket protocol](/websocket/protocol).

## Availability

There is **no formal SLA yet**. Our target is **99.5% monthly availability**, and incidents and their resolution are published on the status page. If you need contractual commitments, [contact us](mailto:chat@coinversa.ai).
